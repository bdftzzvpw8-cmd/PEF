Smart Cooler Placement Agreement

This Smart Cooler Placement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of (the "Effective Date") between the parties below.

Operator

Host
Key terms. Operator places AI smart cooler(s) (the "Equipment") at the Host's location (the "Location") at no cost to Host. Operator installs, stocks and services the Equipment. Host receives a commission of % of Net Sales, paid monthly with a statement. Initial term: from the Installation Date. The first 90 days are a trial period with no commission. After that, commission is paid only for months with $1,000 or more in sales. Custom wrap (Section 9): , wrap cost $ plus a $200 installation fee, recovered from commission before any is paid.

1. Placement and Access

Host grants Operator the right to place and operate the Equipment at a spot at the Location agreed by both parties in writing (email is sufficient). Host will provide, at no charge, the floor space, a standard grounded 110V electrical outlet within reach of the Equipment, and reasonable access during the Location's normal hours for installation, stocking, service and removal. Host will not move the Equipment without Operator's consent.

2. Operator's Responsibilities

  1. Deliver and install the Equipment at Operator's expense within a reasonable time after the Effective Date. The date the Equipment first operates at the Location is the "Installation Date."
  2. Stock the Equipment at least weekly with a product mix chosen by Operator with Host's input, and keep the Equipment clean and in good working order. Operator will respond to service issues within two business days.
  3. Handle all payment processing, customer refunds and chargebacks. Host has no responsibility for sales, cash or customer transactions.
  4. Maintain commercial general liability insurance covering the Equipment and Operator's activities at the Location, with a certificate provided on request.

3. Commission

Operator will pay Host a commission of ____ percent (__%) of Net Sales from the Equipment (the "Commission"). "Net Sales" means gross sales collected from the Equipment less sales tax, refunds, chargebacks and payment-processing fees. Operator will pay the Commission within fifteen (15) days after the end of each calendar month, with a statement showing that month's sales. Payment is by check or electronic transfer to the payee Host designates in writing. Host may request supporting sales reports up to twice per year. No Commission is payable for any period before Host has provided its payout details and a completed IRS Form W-9 to Operator.

Trial Period and Monthly Minimum. The first ninety (90) days after the Installation Date are a trial period (the "Trial Period"). No Commission is earned or payable on sales made during the Trial Period. After the Trial Period, Commission is earned for a calendar month only if gross sales from the Equipment in that month are at least one thousand dollars ($1,000) (the "Monthly Minimum"). No Commission is earned for any month in which gross sales are below the Monthly Minimum, and sales do not carry forward between months. For the month in which the Trial Period ends, only sales after the Trial Period count, and the Monthly Minimum is prorated by the days remaining in that month.

4. Term and Renewal

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years from the Installation Date (the "Initial Term"). It then renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. Termination

  1. Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach.
  2. Operator may terminate on thirty (30) days' written notice if the Equipment's sales do not support continued service, if the Location closes or relocates, or if the Equipment is repeatedly damaged or tampered with at the Location.
  3. Host may terminate on sixty (60) days' written notice if Host permanently closes the Location or sells the business, and will make reasonable efforts to have a buyer assume this Agreement.
  4. Except as stated in this Section 5, neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience during the Initial Term.
  5. On expiration or termination, Operator will remove the Equipment within fourteen (14) days and pay any Commission then owed. Host will allow access for removal.

6. Ownership of Equipment

The Equipment, its contents and all sales proceeds remain Operator's property at all times. Host will not sell, move, alter, encumber or permit any lien on the Equipment, and will not open, restock or service it. Host will use reasonable care to protect the Equipment from damage and will notify Operator promptly of any damage, malfunction, theft or vandalism. Host is responsible for damage caused by Host, its employees or contractors.

7. Exclusivity

During the term, Host will not place, or allow a third party to place, any other vending machine, smart cooler, micro-market or unattended retail unit selling beverages or snacks at the Location without Operator's written consent. This does not restrict Host's own staffed food or beverage service.

8. Liability

Each party is responsible for losses caused by its own negligence or willful misconduct, including that of its employees and contractors, and will indemnify the other against third-party claims to that extent. Operator is responsible for the products sold from the Equipment. Neither party is liable to the other for lost profits or indirect or consequential damages, except for indemnity obligations or a breach of Section 6, 7 or 9.

9. Optional Custom Wrap

If Host elects a custom wrap in the Key Terms, Operator will produce and install a vinyl wrap on the Equipment featuring Host's branding. Operator's cost of the wrap plus a two hundred dollar ($200) installation fee (together, the "Wrap Costs") are recovered from Host's Commission: all Commission otherwise earned is retained by Operator until the Wrap Costs are fully recovered, and Commission is paid to Host only after that. Each monthly statement will show the balance remaining. If this Agreement ends before the Wrap Costs are recovered, Host will pay the unrecovered balance within thirty (30) days, except where Operator terminates under Section 5(b). Host will approve a design proof in writing and is responsible for its content; the payment panel, product window and a small Operator mark remain unobstructed. Host grants Operator a limited license to reproduce Host's name, logo and marks on the wrap and in photographs of the installed Equipment, and warrants that it holds the rights to grant that license. The wrap is part of the Equipment and remains Operator's property. Damage beyond normal wear caused by Host, its employees, contractors or invitees is repaired at Host's cost, and re-wraps requested by Host are new Wrap Costs.

10. General

  1. Notices. Written notices go to the addresses above (email is sufficient) and are effective on receipt.
  2. Assignment. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties' successors and permitted assigns. Host will not assign it without Operator's consent, except to a buyer of the Location's business that assumes it in writing.
  3. Relationship. The parties are independent contractors. Nothing here creates a partnership, joint venture, lease or employment relationship.
  4. Governing law. Tennessee law governs this Agreement. The courts located in Williamson County, Tennessee have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
  5. Entire agreement; electronic signatures. This is the entire agreement on its subject and replaces prior discussions and proposals. Changes must be in a writing signed by both parties. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. The parties agree to sign electronically; a signature applied in this document or delivered by email or PDF is as effective as an original.

The parties have signed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
